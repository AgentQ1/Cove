Delete your Inditalk account

Last updated 27 July 2026

You can delete your Inditalk account and its data from inside the app. You do not need to contact us first.

Steps

  1. Open Inditalk.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Type the confirmation shown on screen and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and phone-number registration.
  • Your profile — display name, About text and profile photo.
  • Your encryption keys and chat membership.
  • Files you uploaded, including attachments, status media and your profile photo.
  • All Inditalk data stored on your device.

What does not get deleted

Messages you already sent to other people remain on their devices, in the same way a sent SMS does. We cannot remove those.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If your account is deleted, registering the same phone number again creates a new, empty account.

If you cannot open the app

Email moomolab@gmail.com from the phone number registered to the account, and we will delete it for you.
