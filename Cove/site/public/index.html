Inditalk

A private messenger for texting, voice and video calls with the people you actually talk to.

Inditalk lets you send messages, photos, videos, voice notes, documents and your location, place one-to-one voice and video calls, create groups, and post updates that disappear after 24 hours. You sign in with your phone number — no email address and no password.

Your controls

  • Choose who can see your last seen, profile photo, About text and read receipts.
  • Block or report any contact directly from their profile.
  • Turn on disappearing messages for any chat.
  • Delete your account and its data at any time from Settings.
