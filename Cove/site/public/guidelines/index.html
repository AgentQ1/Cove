Community Guidelines

Last updated 27 July 2026

Inditalk is for talking to people you know. These rules apply to everything you send: messages, photos, videos, voice notes, group names and status updates.

Not allowed

  • Content that sexualises minors. Zero tolerance. Accounts are removed immediately and reported to the appropriate authorities.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats or stalking, including coordinating others to target someone.
  • Hate speech — attacking people based on religion, caste, ethnicity, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • Violent extremism or incitement to violence.
  • Non-consensual intimate images, or sharing someone's private information without their permission.
  • Spam — bulk or automated unsolicited messaging, scams, phishing, or fraudulent financial schemes.
  • Impersonation of another person or organisation.
  • Illegal goods and services, including the sale of drugs, weapons or stolen data.
  • Content you do not have the right to share, including copyrighted material.

How to report

Open the contact's profile, or a group's info screen, and tap Report. Reports are sent to our safety team and include enough context to act on. You can also email moomolab@gmail.com.

How to block

Open the contact's profile and tap Block. A blocked contact cannot send you messages or call you, and is not told that you blocked them. You can unblock at any time from Settings → Privacy → Blocked Contacts.

What we do about reports

We aim to review every report within 24 hours. Depending on what we find we may remove content, warn the account, suspend it, or remove it permanently. Content that sexualises minors is actioned immediately and referred to the appropriate authorities.

If you believe we got a decision wrong, reply to us at moomolab@gmail.com and we will look again.

Emergencies

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. We cannot respond to emergencies.
